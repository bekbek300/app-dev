Why I Love This Series

I love Fireworks of My Heart because it shows that true love requires patience, understanding, and sacrifice. I admire Song Yan because he is brave, hardworking, and willing to risk his safety to save other people. I also like Xu Qin because she is a dedicated doctor who cares about her patients. Their story teaches me that love is not always easy, especially when family problems and misunderstandings get in the way. I also enjoy the emotional scenes and the chemistry between the main characters. This series reminds me that we should not give up easily on the things and people that truly matter to us.

My favorite Character

My favorite character in Fireworks of My Heart is Song Yan, played by Yang Yang. I admire him because he is not only a brave firefighter but also a responsible leader who always puts the safety of others first.
